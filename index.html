Each of Us, All of Us

FRIDAY, DECEMBER 11
6:00 - 9:00 PM
APPLIED ARTS 221E

A Graphic Design Showcase of Universes Within a Collective

-cosm, a prefix variant meaning universe. Inspired by the macrocosm and microcosm, a concept that describes the historical and philosophical view that a human being or a small system (the microcosm) mirrors the structure and pattern of a larger whole, a universe or society (the macrocosm).

COSM as an exhibition highlights how our group of 17 designers with focuses and identities directly mirrors a larger whole of society.
